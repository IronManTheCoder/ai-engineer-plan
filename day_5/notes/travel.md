# Japan trip notes

Planning a two-week trip through Japan in the fall. Tokyo first for a few days,
then the bullet train down to Kyoto for the temples and gardens.

Want to try a proper kaiseki dinner at least once, and spend a full day just
wandering Nara to see the deer park. Need to book the JR Pass before arriving,
it's cheaper that way.

Osaka is supposed to have the best street food in the country — takoyaki and
okonomiyaki are the two things everyone recommends.
